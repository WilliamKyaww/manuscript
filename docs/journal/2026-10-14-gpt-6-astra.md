## Research and interpretation

lists browser configuration, network conditions and detection outcomes as possible causes. It explicitly says
that the DNS probes and a Private Access Token 401 can be normal. Therefore changing DNS, weakening security or
treating all child-frame errors as fatal is not justified by these observations.

[Supported environments](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-challenges/reference/supported-browsers/)
gives embedded browsers limited support and does not support automated browsers for solving production
challenges. The agent's Playwright-launched observation establishes rendering only. It cannot certify successful
verification in the user's normally launched application or conclusively identify the cause of their loop.
