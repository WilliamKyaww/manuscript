# The study loop

Manuscript is built around one loop, and each step feeds the next:

```text
lecture → concepts → graph → study materials → practice → mastery → weak concepts → targeted study → better notes
```

Everything below works with the built-in test model for trying it out, and with a real model once you
have set one up ([AI](./ai.md)). Without AI you can still do every step except the ones that need a model
to write for you (steps 2 and 3 can be done by hand).

## 1. Bring the lecture in

Import the slides or documents ([Lecture material](./sources.md)), create the module and the lecture
([Your course](./course.md)), and attach the material to the lecture.

## 2. Make study materials

On the lecture's page, **Make study materials…** takes the lecture to a summary note, concepts and
links, flashcards, questions and a quiz in one go ([Generating](./generation.md)). All of it waits in the
**Inbox**. Accept the **concepts** first, then make the **cards** again so that they know the concepts.

## 3. See how it connects

Accepted concepts and relations appear in the **Graph** ([The knowledge graph](./graph.md)): colour it by
mastery to see what you know, and open **Between modules** to see where one course meets another. State
which concepts are **prerequisites** for which: mastery uses it.

## 4. Practise

Review the cards, take the quiz, use **Learn** ([Flashcards](./flashcards.md), [Practice](./practice.md)),
or ask the tutor to quiz you ([Chat](./chat.md)). Every review and answer is evidence.

## 5. See where you stand

**Progress** shows mastery for every concept with the sum behind it, including credit from concepts that
build on it ([How mastery is worked out](./mastery.md)). Weak areas come with a button to practise — or,
for a concept with nothing to practise yet, to make questions from your material.

## 6. Let it show up in the right place

- **Plan** sets aside time for what is due and what is weakest ([The study plan](./planning.md)).
- **Today** shows today's plan and, just before a lecture, an exam or a deadline, **the weak spots of
  that module and the notes to reread**.
- When you finish a quiz, each mistake on a concept with a note becomes a suggestion to add it to that
  note under *Common mistakes*.

## What stays in your hands

Nothing here changes your notes, cards or questions without your say-so: it all arrives as drafts and
suggestions you accept, edit or reject, and what the assistant does for you can be undone. Mastery is
only ever worked out from what you review and answer.
